# Asset spec for "Lane Rush" (Godot 4 lane shooter)

All assets are generated by Blender python scripts (Blender 5.2, run headless:
`blender -b --python tools/blender/<script>.py`) and exported as **GLB** to `assets/models/`.
Low-poly, stylised, clean silhouettes, saturated but tasteful colours ("Playrix/Voodoo ad" look).

## Global conventions
- 1 Blender unit = 1 metre. Origin of each asset at ground level (z=0) centred.
- Characters **face Blender +Y**. (glTF export turns +Y into Godot -Z = Godot forward.) Up = +Z.
- Export: `bpy.ops.export_scene.gltf(filepath=..., export_format='GLB', export_yup=True, export_apply=True)`
  with ONLY the asset's objects in the scene (fresh scene per asset: `bpy.ops.wm.read_factory_settings(use_empty=True)`).
- Materials: Principled BSDF, base colour only (no textures needed), roughness ~0.7, metallic 0 unless metal.
  Keep material count small; name materials sensibly (e.g. `MAT_skin`, `MAT_uniform`, `MAT_wood`).
- Every mesh gets smooth/flat shading as appropriate (flat for boxy parts), and no loose/degenerate geometry.
- Keep each GLB < 1 MB. Target polycounts: characters ≤ 3k tris, props ≤ 1.5k.
- Object names in the GLB must match the names below EXACTLY (Godot code looks them up by name).

## Characters (procedurally animated in Godot, so they must be split into parts)
Hierarchy (parent → child). Each part is a separate mesh object whose **object origin is at its joint pivot**:

```
<Root>              (empty, at ground, origin 0,0,0)
└─ Hips             (mesh: pelvis/belt; origin at hip height ~0.95 m)
   ├─ Torso         (mesh: chest + vest; origin at bottom of torso = hip height)
   │  ├─ Head       (mesh: head + helmet/cap; origin at neck ~1.5 m)
   │  ├─ ArmL       (mesh: full left arm; origin at left shoulder, hangs down)
   │  └─ ArmR       (mesh: full right arm; origin at right shoulder)
   │     └─ Gun     (soldier only; mesh: rifle held in right hand, barrel pointing +Y)
   │        └─ Muzzle   (EMPTY at barrel tip, +Y)
   ├─ LegL          (mesh: full left leg; origin at left hip, points down to ground)
   └─ LegR          (mesh: full right leg; origin at right hip)
```
Rest pose = standing straight, arms down (soldier: arms hold the rifle forward at chest height, ~pointing +Y;
zombie: BOTH arms raised straight forward at shoulder height, classic zombie pose).
Left = Blender -X, Right = +X.

1. `soldier.glb`   Root name `Soldier`. ~1.8 m tall, chunky heroic proportions (big head, big hands),
   green/olive uniform, tan tactical vest, helmet, dark boots, rifle (`Gun`) with a visible barrel tip.
2. `zombie.glb`    Root name `Zombie`. ~1.75 m, slightly hunched, grey-green skin, torn blue shirt/brown pants,
   dark hollow eyes (small dark spheres/cubes), both arms forward.
3. `zombie_brute.glb` Root name `Zombie`. Same hierarchy, ~2.4 m, very wide torso, big arms, darker skin, red eyes.

## Props / world (single-mesh or a few meshes, any internal names, root at ground)
4. `road_segment.glb`  Asphalt road **8 m wide (x: -4..4) × 20 m long (y: 0..20)**, tileable along Y.
   Includes: dark asphalt slab (slightly below z=0, top at z=0), dashed centre line at x=0 (yellow/white),
   light grey curbs + sidewalks from |x|=4 to |x|=6 (sidewalk top at z=0.15), and a few cracks/patches as
   darker quads. No objects beyond |x|=6. Must tile seamlessly (same cross-section at y=0 and y=20).
5. `building_a.glb`, `building_b.glb`, `building_c.glb`  Low-poly post-apocalyptic city buildings, footprint
   ~8 m × 8 m (x: -4..4, y: -4..4), heights 10 / 16 / 24 m, with window insets (darker quads), boarded windows,
   different muted colours (brick red, concrete grey, dusty beige). Front faces -X (they'll sit on the right side)
   — not critical, they are symmetric enough.
6. `car_wreck.glb`   Burnt-out sedan ~4.5 m × 2 m, rusty orange/grey, no wheels needed beyond simple cylinders.
7. `barrier.glb`     Concrete jersey barrier 2 m long with yellow/black stripe.
8. `lamp_post.glb`   6 m tall grey lamp post with a lamp head (separate emissive-looking light-yellow material `MAT_lamp`).
9. `crate.glb`       Military supply crate, 1.0 m cube centred at x=0,y=0, z from 0 to 1.0, olive green wood
   with a metal edge frame and a **flat, slightly lighter square panel on the -Y face** (the face towards the
   player) where Godot draws the number. Root name `Crate`.
10. `powerup.glb`    A floating pickup: a glossy golden-yellow capsule/star-ish shape ~0.6 m, root at z=0, mesh
    centred at z=0.6, material `MAT_gold` (metallic 0.8, roughness 0.3). Root name `Powerup`.
11. `tree_dead.glb`  Leafless dead tree ~5 m, dark brown.
12. `sandbag.glb`    A low sandbag wall 2 m wide, 0.8 m tall, tan.

## Preview renders
For every asset, also render a 512×512 preview PNG (Workbench or EEVEE, 3/4 view, light background) to the
directory given by env var `PREVIEW_DIR` so a human can sanity check silhouettes and colours.
